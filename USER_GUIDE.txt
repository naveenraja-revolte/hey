EVERYDAY CALCULATOR — QUICK GUIDE

Use the keypad or your keyboard to enter a calculation. Chained operations are evaluated as you go, from left to right. For example, 2 + 3 × 4 gives 20.

KEYBOARD CONTROLS

0–9          Enter a digit
.            Add a decimal point
+ or -       Add or subtract
* or /       Multiply or divide
Enter or =   Show the result
Backspace    Delete the last character in the current number
Escape       Clear the calculation
Delete       Clear the calculation

Use the on-screen ± button to change the sign of the current number. AC clears the calculation, and ⌫ removes the last character. If you try to divide by zero, the calculator explains the error; enter a new digit or clear the calculation to continue.

Calculations stay in the current page and are not saved.

A warm-hearted campus comedy

3 Idiots

2009 · Hindi · Directed by Rajkumar Hirani

8 out of 10

The review

Funny, generous, and full of restless energy, 3 Idiots turns the pressure of college life into a story about friendship and finding your own way. Its jokes land easily, but the film's warmth is what stays with you.

Aamir Khan, R. Madhavan, and Sharman Joshi make the central trio feel like old friends, while the campus setting gives the comedy room to swing between absurd fun and more sincere moments. The film can be broad, but its heart is in the right place: curiosity matters more than chasing a perfect score.

A crowd-pleaser with a kind message and enough laughs to earn its long runtime.

Why give it a watch?

  • The friendship A lively trio whose bond gives the story its center.
  • The humor Big comic moments balanced with warm, human details.
  • The message An encouraging nudge to follow curiosity, not just pressure.
